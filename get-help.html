If your child is enrolled or eligible for support, here is how the process works.

Medical emergencies

If your child is experiencing a medical emergency, seek urgent medical care immediately. Foundation support processes should never be understood as a substitute for emergency treatment.

Who qualifies

Every child formally enrolled in our programme receives a secure, continuously updated care and support record. Eligibility considers documented Sickle Cell Disease diagnosis, financial or social vulnerability, and the specific support requested.

How a request works

  • 1. Register

    A caregiver registers with the Foundation and provides basic identifying and contact information.

  • 2. Assess

    A case worker reviews your child's situation and identifies the support that is genuinely needed.

  • 3. Verify

    We verify eligibility using appropriate documentation, proportionate to the support requested.

  • 4. Coordinate

    We connect your child with a verified provider and arrange direct payment wherever possible.

  • 5. Follow up

    We confirm delivery and track what your child needs next.

Report a safeguarding concern

If you are worried about a child's safety - including a child not yet enrolled with us - you can report a concern. You do not need to confront the person you are concerned about.
