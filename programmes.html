Our Programmes

Our Programmes

Six areas of coordinated support, each following the same principle: need, assessment, intervention, delivery, follow-up.

  • Medication Support

    Coordinating access to clinician-prescribed medication through verified pharmacies. We fund access to treatment - we never independently prescribe or alter a prescription.

  • Medical Care

    Hospital visits, specialist referrals, and diagnostic or monitoring services, coordinated with our partner healthcare facilities.

  • Nutrition

    Household food support and, where clinically indicated, approved nutritional interventions recommended by a qualified professional.

  • Education

    School fees, learning materials, and uniform support - because a child's health condition should not become the reason they leave school.

  • Rehabilitation

    Physiotherapy and other approved rehabilitation services where clinically indicated and referred.

  • Emergency Support

    Exceptional urgent assistance coordinated alongside - never instead of - appropriate emergency medical care.

How support is funded

Every programme is supported through one of four donation pathways: sponsoring a specific child, sponsoring a whole programme, funding a specific identified need, or a general donation the Foundation allocates according to priority.
