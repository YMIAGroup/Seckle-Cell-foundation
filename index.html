We don't simply pay bills.

When a child needs help, we look beyond the immediate cost. We identify what is needed, coordinate the right support, connect the child with an approved provider, track delivery, and follow up.

  • Identify

    A caregiver, healthcare provider, or community referral brings a child's need to our attention.

  • Assess

    A case worker determines what kind of support is genuinely needed - medical, nutritional, educational, or otherwise.

  • Coordinate

    We connect the child with a verified provider - a hospital, pharmacy, school, or therapist we already work with.

  • Follow up

    We confirm the support was delivered, and track what the child needs next.

Whole-child support, not just treatment.

Sickle Cell Disease affects far more than a child's health. Our support is organised around five pillars.

Care

Medical access, medication, and healthcare coordination.

Nourish

Nutrition and household food support.

Learn

Education continuity - school fees, materials, and support.

Thrive

Rehabilitation, psychosocial and long-term wellbeing.

Connect

Coordination between caregivers, providers, donors and community.

Transparency you can trust.

Every donation is tracked from the moment it arrives to the moment it becomes medication, a meal, or a school fee. We publish aggregated impact - never a child's private information.
