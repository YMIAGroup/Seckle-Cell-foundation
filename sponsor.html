What a sponsor sees

When you sponsor a named child, you receive an approved profile - enough to feel genuinely connected, without exposing the child's private medical or family information.

You will never see: the child's full name, exact address, school, phone number, or detailed medical records. The relationship is a financial one - you are not the child's guardian, and you cannot contact them directly.
