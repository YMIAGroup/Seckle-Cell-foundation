Donation → Fund → Allocation → Delivery

Every donation is tracked through our internal financial ledger. Restricted donations are held to their designated purpose - a gift restricted to medication cannot silently become school fees.

  • Received

    Your donation enters the relevant fund - general, or restricted to a specific programme.

  • Allocated

    The Foundation identifies an eligible need matching your fund's restriction.

  • Paid

    Funds are paid directly to the verified provider - a pharmacy, hospital, or school.

  • Verified

    The provider confirms delivery, and where appropriate, the caregiver confirms receipt.

Honest reporting

We are transparent about what we can and cannot yet do. Our Nairobi pilot is intentionally small - we are proving that our care-coordination model works reliably before expanding.
