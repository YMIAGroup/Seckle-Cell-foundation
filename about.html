A care coordination foundation, not a charity that pays medical bills.

We are a Kenyan foundation dedicated to ensuring that vulnerable children living with Sickle Cell Disease do not fall through the gaps between family, healthcare, financing and community support.

Our operating principle

Most organisations ask: "How much money does this child need?" We ask something different.

What does this child need now, who is responsible for providing it, how will it be funded, has it been delivered, when does it need to happen again, and what is the next action?

Where we work

Our long-term vision is nationwide coverage across Kenya. We are deliberately starting small.

  • Nairobi County

    Our active pilot. Every part of our care-coordination model is being tested here first - enrolment, caregiver support, provider coordination, direct payments, and follow-up.

  • Future counties

    We expand only once we can demonstrate that the Foundation reliably coordinates a child's support from need identification through delivery and follow-up.

Governance & accountability

Because we handle children's sensitive information and donor funds, no single person controls the full beneficiary, funding, and payment cycle.

Direct provider payment

By default, we pay hospitals and pharmacies directly rather than handing funds to caregivers - this is one of our strongest financial controls against misuse.

Independent safeguarding

Child protection decisions are never controlled solely by whoever is delivering a programme. Safeguarding concerns are reviewed independently.
